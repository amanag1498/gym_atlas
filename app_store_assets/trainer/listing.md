## Description

Gym Atlas Trainer gives coaches a focused workspace for the members assigned by
their gym.

Review member context, build and assign workouts, create flexible meal-based
diet plans, organize follow-ups, monitor progress, and keep coaching
conversations connected to each client.

Key features:

- Assigned-member overview and member detail
- Today's clients and pending follow-ups
- Workout plan, template and exercise management
- Meal-based diet-plan creation and assignment
- Attendance, progress and workout-log visibility
- Private trainer notes and follow-up completion
- Direct member chat and push notifications
- Trainer profile and user-selected certification uploads
- Privacy, support and account-deletion controls

Access requires an active Gym Atlas trainer account and permissions assigned by
a participating gym.
